# Excel Sales Business Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze Vrinda Store's annual sales performance, customer demographics, order fulfillment, and sales channel contribution.

## Dashboard Features
- **Monthly Sales Analysis:** Compare monthly revenue and order volume.
- **Customer Demographics:** Analyze sales distribution between men and women.
- **Order Status Analysis:** Track delivered, cancelled, returned, and refunded orders.
- **Regional Performance:** Compare sales across states.
- **Age Group Analysis:** Examine purchasing patterns by age group and gender.
- **Sales Channel Analysis:** Compare contributions from Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, and other channels.
- **Interactive Filters:** Explore results by month, sales channel, and product category.

## Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
- Slicers and interactive filtering
- Data aggregation and visualization
